Energy Report Generator

Fill in the details from the customer's electricity bill, then download the PDF report.

1. Customer details

Found at the top of the electricity bill.

Name of the consumer as printed on the bill.
Full address including PIN code.
Unique ID of the connection, printed near the top of the bill. 8-15 letters/digits. It is also used as the Report ID.

2. Meter and roof

Check "Phase" / connection type on the bill. Most homes are single-phase.
Open roof area where solar panels can be placed, without shade from buildings or trees.

3. Last 6 months consumption

Copy from the "consumption history" table on the bill, latest month first. Fill at least 3 months.

Units = electricity used in that month. Bill amount = total amount billed for that month. If the bill history does not show amounts, use each month's "Net payable" amount.
